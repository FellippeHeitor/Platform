## Description (paste into the description box)

---

**Tap to hop, hold to jump higher. Don't stop, don't fall.**

Leap Line is a small one-button platformer. The hero runs by itself and the only thing you control is the jump: a tap gives a short hop, holding gives a higher jump. The 11 levels are hand-made and the same every time, so you learn each one by dying and trying again. Retrying is instant, and the game shows how far you got.

**Jam theme:** THEME
**Made with:** QB64 Phoenix Edition and QBJS (the same BASIC source runs in both)

### Controls

- **Space**, mouse button or touch: jump
- **R**: restart the attempt
- **M**: mute
- **Esc**: back to the title screen
- On the title screen, **Left/Right** picks any level you've already played, and **F** turns on free mode: walk with the arrows, beat the clock and collect stars for a score

### What's in it

- 11 levels across 5 themes: meadow, dusk, desert, night and jungle
- Spikes, low ceilings, planks you can jump up through, diamonds that give you one extra jump in mid-air, switches, teleporters
- A jungle level with swinging vines and crocodiles
- Jetpack and wings power-ups in the last five levels
- After each death, a dotted line marks where your last attempt ended

Every sound, the music and all the graphics are generated by the program itself: no image or audio files were drawn or recorded. Every level was checked by a solver that plays it through the game's own physics.

### Downloads

- **Browser:** play above. Progress is kept only while the page is open.
- **macOS** (Apple Silicon, macOS 11 or later): unzip and open *Leap Line.app*. It isn't notarized, so the first time, right-click it and choose **Open**, then **Open** again (or allow it under System Settings > Privacy & Security). Your progress is saved in `~/Library/Application Support/Leap Line`.
